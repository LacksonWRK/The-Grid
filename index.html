HOW TO PLAY

1 · DRAW A LINEOn your turn, drag from a dot to the next dot (or just tap a line). Straight lines only.

2 · CLOSE A SQUAREDraw the 4th side of a square and it becomes yours. One line can close two squares!

3 · NO BONUS TURNTurns always pass to the next player — even after you capture a square. Think ahead!

4 · COMBO SCORINGOptional mode: touching squares of one color form a group worth N×N points. Build big!

5 · WALLSGrey steel walls are already built. They count as a side, so those squares need fewer lines.

6 · MOST WINSWhen every line is drawn, the highest score wins. Ties are shared.
